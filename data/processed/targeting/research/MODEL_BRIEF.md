# MODEL BRIEF for content writers (Change Bloc Field Atlas)

Context: Israel's 26th Knesset election is 27 Oct 2026 (today 11 Oct 2026, 16 days to go). The site is a locality/polling-station targeting atlas for the 'change bloc' (Yashar/Eisenkot, Together = Bennett 2026 + Yesh Atid, The Democrats/Golan, Yisrael Beytenu/Liberman). Netanyahu bloc (NB) = Likud, Shas, UTJ, Otzma, RZP-Zehut, Amcha, Noam, Haredi Public. Arab lists = Ra'am, Joint List (Balad+Hadash-Ta'al). Unaligned, below threshold: Blue and White (Gantz, ~0.6%), Reservists-Economic (Hendel/Zelekha, ~1.6%, pass prob 0.20).
Forecast (8-10 Oct): Likud 27, Yashar 23, Together 11, Democrats 9, Joint List 9, UTJ 8, Shas 8, YB 8, Otzma 7, Ra'am 5 (pass .78), RZP-Zehut 5 (pass .78). P(NB>=61)=21.7%; P(change alone>=61)=5.2%; with Ra'am 18.8%; P(change-led government, formation model)=18.2%; repeat election 41.9%.

MODEL (what the site computes):
- Data: official CEC results by locality for 7 elections (2013-2022) and by polling station (kalpi) for 2022; CBS locality file 2023 (coordinates, type), 2022 Census by locality; CBS SES cluster.
- 2026 projection for every locality and kalpi: 2022 votes carried through the forecast's lineage composition (which 2022 electorates each 2026 list draws from) and raked (IPF) to the forecast's national shares (adjusted for double-envelope votes). Backtest of the method on 2021->2022: locality camp-share MAE 1.4-1.5pp vs 1.8-2.2pp for uniform swing.
- Three field mechanisms, per completed contact:
  * GOTV (turnout): 1.5pp turnout lift per completed door conversation at baseline, times a hump in baseline turnout (peak at 30-55% resident turnout, x0.5 at 70-80%, x0.25 above 80%), x1.3 household spillover. Net value depends on WHO is mobilised (the locality's/kalpi's projected vote: mobilising in Netanyahu-majority turf nets NB votes). Only positive-value polling-station areas are used ('turf selection').
  * Persuasion: cross-bloc switching ~0.3pp per conversation with a soft NB voter; persuadable universe = soft NB supporters (1 - certainty) x 22% cross-pressured, localised by an 'openness' index (volatility, soft-right footprint, bloc balance; validated out of sample: Spearman 0.38, top/bottom quintile movement ratio ~3.1-3.4). Scaled down in haredi (x0.15), national-religious settlements (x0.35), RZ inside Green Line (x0.6); zero in Arab localities. National persuadable ~2.0% of voters (~86k).
  * Wasted-vote rescue: 5% of contacted Blue & White / Reservists voters move to a viable list, 60% of them to a change list. Universe ~2.1% of voters (~91k).
- Exchange rate: marginal value of one vote per list from perturbing the forecast's 8,000 election-day share draws through the full seat law (threshold, surplus pairs, Bader-Ofer) and the government-formation model. Per 10,000 votes: Yashar +0.50pp P(change-led govt), Democrats +0.50, Together +0.49, YB +0.39, Likud -0.46, Otzma -0.58, RZP-Zehut -0.70, Shas -0.37, UTJ -0.26, Ra'am -0.03 (but +0.82pp on blocking), Joint List -0.41 (but +0.75 on blocking). KEY TRADE-OFF: Arab votes deny Netanyahu 61 but under the formation model mostly convert NB wins into deadlock/repeat elections, while diluting change-list seats, so they are ~0 or negative for a change-led government and strongly positive for blocking.
- Outputs: 'potential' (net votes at saturation = 35% of eligible in positive turf), efficiency (net votes per 1,000 contacts), per 100 volunteer-hours, indices (percentiles), tiers: Turnout engine, Persuasion battleground, Secondary target, Blocking-partner zone, Opposition stronghold, Low yield.
- A full saturation field campaign = ~4,400 net votes for change-led-govt objective (~0.1 seat; +0.2pp P(change govt)); blocking objective ~12,400 net votes (+0.7pp P(NB<61)). Field work is a margin lever; 2022 was decided by ~30k votes and threshold failures (Meretz missed by ~4k votes).
- Top 10 localities hold 46% of potential; top 25 hold 64%.
