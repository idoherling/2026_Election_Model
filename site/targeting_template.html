Net votes are measured under the objective selected in the bar at the top. Potential is the saturation total: door conversations with 35% of the residents of every polling-station area where a conversation has positive value, plus list calls to identified voters (30% identified, half reached) where it does not; rescue calls run anywhere. Arab localities and polling stations where Arab lists lead are not ranked or planned. Efficiency is net votes per 1,000 completed contacts. Flags: displaced border communities whose residents may not be home; low confidence Druze localities, whose vote depends on 2026 candidates; not targeted access-restricted villages.

Segments

Sixteen electorates, sixteen playbooks

Israel's electoral map is sorted by sector, religiosity and class far more than by region. Each locality is assigned to one segment from its census religion, settlement type and 2022 vote. Each segment needs a different mix of turnout work, persuasion and partner support.

Field · Playbook

Field playbook: what works, when, and where

The field-experiment record is clear about what the last two weeks can and cannot do. Personal contact raises turnout; impersonal contact rarely does. Persuasion across blocs is small this late. Insurance messages to voters of lists below the threshold work best when the final polls show those lists below the line.

Planner

Allocate a field budget

Set how many completed contacts the bloc can deliver before election day and how much of its electorate it has identified. The planner spends the contacts where the next one buys the most: door teams on positive-value polling-station areas, calls to identified voters everywhere else, best first.

Methodology

How the atlas is built

The atlas rests on four steps: harmonised official results, a 2026 projection for every locality and polling station, a field model calibrated to experiments, and an exchange rate taken from the forecast's own simulations. Every choice below was tested out of sample where the data allowed. Where an idea failed that test, we dropped it and say so.
